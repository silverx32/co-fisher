# Board games, untimed turns, turn notice and collapsible friends bar

Goal: Add aeroplane chess (飞行棋), Chinese chess (象棋) and a Monopoly-style game (大富翁) to the room tables; remove every turn countdown while still letting players quit (no refund); notify the player whose turn it is; let the friends bar collapse to show only yourself.

Confirmed decisions:
- No turn timers anywhere, including mahjong's 30 s / 12 s windows. Only a player offline for 60 s gets a stand-in (same safe moves as the old timeout plus simple heuristics for the new games), paced 0.8 s per move. The short pause between blackjack / Hold'em hands remains an automatic, unlabelled pause.
- Quitting after the start is always allowed and forfeits the ticket (existing rule, kept for the new games): xiangqi quitting resigns; monopoly releases the quitter's lots to the bank; flight removes their planes.
- 飞行棋 2–4 players, 4 planes each: take off on a 6, a 6 rolls again, three 6s in a row void the turn; own-colour squares jump 4, square 18 flies to 30; landing on opponents sends them to the hangar; exact roll home with bounce-back. First with all four home takes the prize.
- 象棋 exactly 2 players, first entrant red. Full legality on the server (horse leg, elephant eye, cannon screen, palace, flying general, no self-check). Checkmate or stalemate wins; agreed draw or 60 moves without a capture is a draw that splits the prize.
- 大富翁 2–6 players. Each table generates a new 32-square ring: corners fixed (start, overtime, park, lottery), 20 lots with shuffled names, ±10% prices rising around the ring, consecutive districts of 2–3, and shuffled chance / tax / red-packet squares. Eight original characters, one each: salary +100, rent received +30%, buy at 80%, half-price upgrades, only good chance cards, rent at 60% and no tax, three dice keep the best two, choose the roll once then cool down 3 turns. 1,500 in-game cash; lots upgrade to level 3; a full district doubles rent; shortfalls sell lots at half value, then bankruptcy (settled at zero immediately). After 20 rounds the highest cash + property value wins; ties split.
- Prize stays 80% of all tickets for every game; no wallet-point side bets.
- The table snapshot carries `act` (the table waiting on this player, even while watching another) and `kinds` (games this host supports). The client shows a 5 s top-right notice on the overlay when `act` appears, obeying 免打扰 and full-screen detection. Older hosts omit `kinds`, so the client offers only the original four games there.
- The friends bar toggle lives in the Boss / table-entry row, hides only friend cards, and is remembered locally.

Verification: 55 Rust unit tests (legal-move counts, checkmate, draw flow, flight jumps / captures / bounce, monopoly map invariants, skills, bankruptcy, stand-ins only for away seats, quitting forfeits, full automated games terminate and settle exactly 80%), 23 WebSocket tests through the real relay (including xiangqi turn ownership / move privacy / draw split and monopoly map / unique picks / quitting), the existing two layout checks, `tools/check-board-games.js` (real relay snapshots rendered at 910x720 for all three boards, black's flipped view, controls reachable, whole board scrolled into view) and `tools/check-pet-extras.js` (friends toggle on the real pet.js, overlay notice placement and clean-up). Not yet verified: real multi-desktop play, Steam transport, Docker relay, or a packaged release.
